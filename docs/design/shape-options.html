Shape options

The same slice of GameMaster five ways: the menu, a calendar week, the next game, buttons, nation status and search. Colours and type are the same in all five; only the shapes change. People (avatars) stay round in every option.

APlotting sheet (today's sample)

Squares everywhere. Precise and calm, but flat: a button, a game and a panel all have the same shape.

BField kit

Soft and round, close to Discord. Friendly and easy to read, but it's the shape most apps already have.

CCut plate

Corners clipped like stencilled equipment plates. The most specific to a war game, without being a costume.

DDossier

Panels are file folders with their title on a tab; the open page is a tab too, and today is circled. Reads as staff paperwork.

ELean forward

Anything you press slants; boxes that only hold things stay calm. Fast and game-like, the closest to an esports look.
